Excel Fantasy Basketball Project

An Excel project tracking NBA players’ stats across multiple categories. It calculates standard deviation, player replacement value, variance, and comparisons between fantasy teams. Below is the matchup calculator used in the excel sheet. For every weekly matchup, the sheet will calculate every rostered player's stats from the entire season and the past 2 weeks, count their total games for the week and predict the total points the player will score in the week. It does the same for your opponent's team and gives you multiple helpful tools. Those tools include games per day (10 max), points per day, total games, overlap games, percent chance to win, and estimated total.
